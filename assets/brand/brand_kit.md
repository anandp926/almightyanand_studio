## 5. Monetization Readiness Checklist

To ensure every video qualifies for the YouTube Partner Program (YPP), Meta In-Stream Ads, and Meta Performance Bonus without policy flags:

- [x] **100% Original Content Guarantee:**
  - Every script is custom-written and tailored to Anand's brand voice.
  - No re-uploaded TikToks, TV clips, or movie scenes without transformative fair use.
- [x] **Zero Third-Party Watermarks:**
  - No CapCut, TikTok, InShot, or competitor platform logos/watermarks.
  - Video exports are rendered at native 1080x1920 (9:16) with pristine borders.
- [x] **Acoustic Profiling & Human Voice Timbre:**
  - Audio runs through Anand's acoustic voice profile ($F_0=150$ Hz median) with natural beat-based cadence.
  - Zero robotic speech engine slurs (phonetically mapped: *A-I*, *P-D-F*, *U-P-I*, *Per-plexity*).
  - Background music ducked to $-20\text{ dBFS}$ with EBU R128 master normalization at $-16\text{ LUFS}$.
- [x] **Anti-Hallucination & Legible Subtitles:**
  - Script-anchored subtitle timing leaving $+0.8\text{s}$ clean decay room on CTAs.
  - Zero Devanagari Unicode glyph corruption or mistranscriptions.
- [x] **Advertiser-Friendly Policy Adherence:**
  - No profanity, hate speech, violent imagery, or deceptive get-rich-quick schemes.
  - Clear financial educational disclaimers protecting against SEBI/regulatory misrepresentation.
- [x] **Legal & Payout Preparedness:**
  - PAN Card, Indian Bank Account (with IFSC & SWIFT code for international wire), and Government ID ready for Google AdSense and Meta Payouts when subscriber/follower thresholds are reached.
