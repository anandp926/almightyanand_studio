## Step 4: Monetization Eligibility Milestones & Documents to Prepare

Keep these legal and financial items ready so that the moment you cross platform thresholds, your payouts activate immediately without withholding delays:

### 1. YouTube Partner Program (YPP) Thresholds
- **Tier 1 (Fan Funding / Supers / Memberships):**
  - 500 Subscribers + 3 valid public uploads in last 90 days
  - AND either **3,000 public watch hours** (long-form) OR **3 Million Shorts views** in 90 days.
- **Tier 2 (Full Video Ad Revenue & Shorts Ad Revenue Sharing):**
  - 1,000 Subscribers
  - AND either **4,000 public watch hours** in 12 months OR **10 Million Shorts views** in 90 days.

### 2. Meta Monetization Thresholds (Reels & In-Stream)
- **Instagram:**
  - **Gifts & Badges:** 500+ followers, account active for 30+ days, 18+ years old.
  - **Reels Performance Bonus (Invite-Only):** Maintained through consistent posting of 100% original, watermark-free vertical reels.
- **Facebook In-Stream Ads & Performance Bonus:**
  - 5,000 Page Followers
  - 60,000 total eligible minutes viewed in the last 60 days.
  - 5 active public videos.

### 3. Payout Documents to Have on Hand (For Later Activation)
- [ ] **PAN Card (Permanent Account Number):** Needed for Indian tax compliance and AdSense identity verification.
- [ ] **Aadhaar Card or Passport:** For government identity / address verification.
- [ ] **Bank Account Details:**
  - Account Number & Account Holder Name (must exactly match your PAN and AdSense name).
  - Bank **IFSC Code** (for domestic routing).
  - Bank **SWIFT / BIC Code** (essential for international wire transfers from Google Ireland/Meta US).
- [ ] **US Tax Treaty Exemption (Form W-8BEN):**
  - Filled digitally inside Google AdSense / Meta Payouts once eligible.
  - India has a Double Taxation Avoidance Agreement (DTAA) with the US, which reduces US withholding tax from 30% to 15% on US-viewed traffic when your Indian PAN is submitted.
